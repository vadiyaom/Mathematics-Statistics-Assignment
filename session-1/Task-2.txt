TASK 2: Music Genre Data Classification and Reasoning
=====================================================

Sample Data:
['Pop', 'Rock', 'Jazz', 'Hip-Hop', 'Pop', 'Rock', 'Jazz', 'Pop']

1. Classification:
   Categorical Data (Specifically: Nominal Data / Qualitative Data)

2. Reasoning:
   - The data points are descriptive text labels representing different music genres (qualities/attributes) rather than measurable numerical amounts.
   - There is no inherent numerical value, natural rank, or logical mathematical ordering among genres (e.g., 'Pop' is neither mathematically greater than nor lesser than 'Rock').
   - Arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction, or calculating a mean are meaningless for this data; instead, only frequency counts, proportions, and the mode ('Pop', appearing 3 times) can be determined.
