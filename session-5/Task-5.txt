TASK 5: Sampling Techniques for BookMyShow Movie Booking Habits
================================================================

Objective:
Select a representative sample of users from BookMyShow to study their movie booking habits (e.g., booking advance notice, ticket quantities, genre preferences, and concession spending).

Two chosen sampling methods:
1. Stratified Sampling
2. Systematic Sampling

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Stratified Sampling
----------------------------------------------------------------
How the Method Works:
The target population is divided into distinct, non-overlapping subgroups (called "strata") based on an important characteristic that influences booking habits. A random sample is then drawn from each stratum proportionally.

Application to BookMyShow:
1. Identify the Stratification Characteristic: Divide users into strata based on their "Past Booking Frequency" over the last 6 months:
   - Stratum A (Frequent Moviegoers): Users who booked 4 or more movies (e.g., 20% of user base).
   - Stratum B (Occasional Moviegoers): Users who booked 1 to 3 movies (e.g., 50% of user base).
   - Stratum C (Infrequent / New Users): Users who booked only 1 movie or only browsed (e.g., 30% of user base).
2. Sample Selection:
   For a desired total sample of 1,000 users, randomly draw:
   - 200 users from Stratum A (20%)
   - 500 users from Stratum B (50%)
   - 300 users from Stratum C (30%)
3. Key Benefit:
   Ensures that every segment—from die-hard cinema lovers to occasional festival watchers—is proportionately represented, preventing high-frequency users from dominating the survey results.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. Systematic Sampling
----------------------------------------------------------------
How the Method Works:
Users are selected at regular, fixed numerical intervals (every k-th individual) from an ordered list or live transaction stream, starting from a randomly chosen point.

Application to BookMyShow:
1. Arrange Population: Take the chronological database stream of all ticket transactions completed on BookMyShow over a weekend (e.g., N = 100,000 total bookings ordered by timestamp).
2. Determine Interval (k):
   To collect a sample of n = 1,000 users:
   k = Population (N) / Sample Size (n) = 100,000 / 1,000 = 100.
3. Select Starting Point:
   Randomly pick an initial integer between 1 and 100 (for example, booking #42).
4. Extract the Sample:
   Select every 100th booking from that point onwards:
   - 42nd, 142nd, 242nd, 342nd, ..., 99,942nd booking.
5. Key Benefit:
   Extremely easy and automated to execute on high-volume transactional databases. It provides an evenly distributed sample across all movie showtimes (morning matinees, evening peak shows, and midnight screenings) across multiple cinema chains.
