TASK 3: Why Use Sampling Instead of a Full Census for Swiggy Delivery Times?
=================================================================================

Question:
Imagine you are analyzing food delivery times on Swiggy. Explain in 2-3 sentences why you would use sampling instead of collecting data from every single order ever made.

Explanation (2-3 sentences):
1. Analyzing every single order ever made across hundreds of millions of historical transactions is computationally prohibitive, slow, and expensive in terms of database storage and processing power.
2. Older historical data from years ago is practically obsolete and unhelpful because city road infrastructure, traffic conditions, restaurant kitchens, and dispatch algorithms constantly evolve.
3. A well-designed representative sample of recent orders (e.g., a few thousand orders across peak and non-peak hours) provides practically identical statistical accuracy with high confidence at a tiny fraction of the time and computational cost.
