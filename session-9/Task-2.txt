TASK 2: Fitness Tracker Step Counts & Central Tendency in Normal Distributions
================================================================================

1. Problem Description:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Given an array of 20 daily step counts from your fitness tracker app, calculate 
the mean, median, and mode, and check if they are approximately equal as 
expected in a normal distribution.

Hint: Use numpy.mean(), numpy.median(), and scipy.stats.mode().

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Theoretical Framework: Symmetry in a Normal Distribution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In an ideal continuous Normal Distribution N(mu, sigma^2):
1. Mean   : The arithmetic center of the distribution (sum(x) / N).
2. Median : The 50th percentile dividing the data into two equal halves.
3. Mode   : The point of maximum probability density (highest peak).

Mathematical Identity for Normal Distributions:
                      Mean = Median = Mode

In real-world empirical samples:
Due to minor random sampling fluctuations and measurement granularity, exact 
mathematical equality is rarely observed. However, for data that approximates 
a normal distribution, the three metrics must be **approximately equal**:
                      Mean approx Median approx Mode

If the three metrics diverge substantially:
- Mean > Median > Mode  ==> Right-skewed (positively skewed) distribution.
- Mode > Median > Mean  ==> Left-skewed (negatively skewed) distribution.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Dataset (20-Day Fitness Tracker Step Counts):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Daily steps recorded over 20 consecutive days:
[6800, 7200, 7400, 7600, 7800, 7900, 8000, 8000, 8000, 8000,
 8100, 8100, 8200, 8200, 8400, 8500, 8600, 8800, 9000, 9200]

- Sample Size (N) = 20 days

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Python Implementation (task2_step_counts_symmetry.py):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
```python
import numpy as np
from scipy import stats

# 1. Input step counts array
daily_steps = np.array([
    6800, 7200, 7400, 7600, 7800, 7900, 8000, 8000, 8000, 8000,
    8100, 8100, 8200, 8200, 8400, 8500, 8600, 8800, 9000, 9200
])

# 2. Compute Mean, Median, and Mode
mean_steps   = np.mean(daily_steps)
median_steps = np.median(daily_steps)
mode_steps   = stats.mode(daily_steps).mode

print(f"Mean   : {mean_steps:.2f} steps")
print(f"Median : {median_steps:.2f} steps")
print(f"Mode   : {mode_steps:.2f} steps")

# Check if approximately equal (within 5% tolerance)
diff_mean_median = abs(mean_steps - median_steps)
diff_mean_mode   = abs(mean_steps - mode_steps)

print(f"|Mean - Median| = {diff_mean_median:.2f} ({diff_mean_median / mean_steps * 100:.2f}%)")
print(f"|Mean - Mode|   = {diff_mean_mode:.2f} ({diff_mean_mode / mean_steps * 100:.2f}%)")
```

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Calculation Results & Pairwise Comparison:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Mean (numpy.mean())    : 8,090.00 steps
- Median (numpy.median()): 8,050.00 steps
- Mode (stats.mode())    : 8,000.00 steps (frequency = 4 days)
- Standard Deviation (s) : 582.10 steps

Relative Differences:
- |Mean - Median| = |8090 - 8050| = 40.00 steps (0.49% difference)
- |Mean - Mode|   = |8090 - 8000| = 90.00 steps (1.11% difference)
- |Median - Mode| = |8050 - 8000| = 50.00 steps (0.62% difference)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Conclusion & Verdict:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**YES, the Mean (8,090), Median (8,050), and Mode (8,000) are approximately equal.**

- The maximum variation among the three metrics is only 1.11% (well below the 
  conventional statistical divergence threshold of 5%).
- This tight clustering around ~8,050 steps confirms that the daily step counts 
  exhibit strong central tendency and bell-shaped symmetry, demonstrating that the 
  data aligns with the fundamental characteristics of a normal distribution.
