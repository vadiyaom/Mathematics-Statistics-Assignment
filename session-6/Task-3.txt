TASK 3: A/B Test Design for a Mobile App Feature
=================================================

Chosen Feature & App:
Redesigned Sticky "1-Click UPI Pay" Button on Paytm Checkout Page

-------------------------------------------------
1. Overview of the Experiment:
-------------------------------------------------
Currently, when Paytm users proceed to checkout, they land on a page with multiple payment options (Wallet, Net Banking, UPI, Cards) and must scroll to tap a standard "Proceed to Pay" button. 
The proposed feature introduces a prominent, persistent "1-Click UPI Pay" button at the bottom of the screen displaying the user's primary linked bank account, allowing instant authorization.

-------------------------------------------------
2. Control and Variant Groups:
-------------------------------------------------
- Control Group (Group A - 50% of traffic):
  Users see the existing standard checkout screen requiring manual selection of payment mode followed by tapping "Proceed to Pay".
- Variant Group (Group B - 50% of traffic):
  Users see the redesigned checkout page with the prominent, sticky "1-Click UPI Pay" button pre-configured with their default UPI bank account.

User Allocation:
A randomized 50/50 split across 100,000 active transacting users over a 14-day test period, ensuring both weekday and weekend shopping behaviors are captured.

-------------------------------------------------
3. Metrics to Measure:
-------------------------------------------------
- Primary Metric: Payment Checkout Conversion Rate (%)
  Formula:
     Conversion Rate = (Successfully Completed Transactions / Total Checkout Page Visits) * 100

- Secondary Guardrail Metrics:
  1. Average Checkout Completion Time (in seconds, tracking speed).
  2. Payment Failure / UPI Timeout Rate (%) (ensuring the new button does not introduce technical errors).

-------------------------------------------------
4. Hypotheses:
-------------------------------------------------
- Null Hypothesis (H0):
  The redesigned "1-Click UPI Pay" button has no effect on the checkout conversion rate compared to the existing layout.
  Mathematical Form: H0: p_variant <= p_control (or p_variant = p_control)

- Alternative Hypothesis (H1):
  The redesigned "1-Click UPI Pay" button significantly increases the checkout conversion rate compared to the existing layout.
  Mathematical Form: H1: p_variant > p_control

-------------------------------------------------
5. Decision Rule:
-------------------------------------------------
If the two-sample z-test for proportions yields a p-value < 0.05 (with conversion rate significantly higher in Group B and no spike in failure rates), Paytm will reject H0 and roll out the 1-Click button to 100% of all users.
