TASK 1: Hypotheses for Daily Time Spent on Instagram vs. YouTube
================================================================

Scenario:
You believe that people spend more time on Instagram than on YouTube daily.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Definition of Variables:
----------------------------------------------------------------
- mu_Instagram : Population mean daily time spent on Instagram (in minutes).
- mu_YouTube   : Population mean daily time spent on YouTube (in minutes).

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. Null Hypothesis (H0):
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Verbal Statement:
  There is no significant difference in the mean daily time spent on Instagram compared to YouTube, or people spend less than or equal time on Instagram compared to YouTube daily.
- Mathematical Notation:
  H0: mu_Instagram <= mu_YouTube
  (or H0: mu_Instagram - mu_YouTube <= 0)
  (Conventionally also written as: H0: mu_Instagram = mu_YouTube)

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. Alternative Hypothesis (H1 or Ha):
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Verbal Statement:
  People spend significantly more time on Instagram than on YouTube daily.
- Mathematical Notation:
  H1: mu_Instagram > mu_YouTube
  (or H1: mu_Instagram - mu_YouTube > 0)

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. Type of Statistical Test:
----------------------------------------------------------------
This is a **One-Tailed (Right-Tailed) Two-Sample Hypothesis Test**, because the researcher's claim specifies a clear directional difference ("more time on Instagram").
