TASK 1: Swiggy Daily Orders Distribution & Skewness Analysis
=============================================================

1. Given Dataset (10 Days of Swiggy Orders):
   [12, 15, 17, 14, 13, 16, 200, 18, 14, 15]

-------------------------------------------------------------
2. Statistical Calculations:
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Total Orders = 12 + 15 + 17 + 14 + 13 + 16 + 200 + 18 + 14 + 15 = 334
- Number of Days (n) = 10
- Mean (Average) = 334 / 10 = 33.4 orders

Sorted Dataset:
   [12, 13, 14, 14, 15, 15, 16, 17, 18, 200]
- Median (Middle value) = (15 + 15) / 2 = 15.0 orders
- Mode (Most frequent)  = 14 and 15 (each appears 2 times)

Relationship: Mean (33.4) > Median (15.0)

-------------------------------------------------------------
3. Rough Distribution Graph (ASCII Dot / Frequency Plot):
-------------------------------------------------------------

Visual Plot File:
   A high-resolution chart has also been generated and saved as:

-------------------------------------------------------------
4. Classification:
-------------------------------------------------------------
The data distribution is **RIGHT-SKEWED (Positively Skewed)**.

-------------------------------------------------------------
5. Reasoning Based on the Shape:
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. Visual Tail: 9 out of 10 data points are concentrated heavily on the left side between 12 and 18 orders. A single extreme outlier (200 orders on Day 7) stretches a long, thin tail far to the right.
2. Mathematical Pull: Because the arithmetic mean incorporates all values, the extreme outlier pulls the mean (33.4) significantly to the right of the median (15.0).
3. Whenever Mean > Median and the tail extends toward higher positive values, the distribution is classified as right-skewed.
