6. One-Line Justification:
----------------------------------------------------------
The distribution is left-skewed because ratings cluster heavily at the high end (5 stars) while the tail stretches toward the lower ratings on the left, pulling the Mean (4.3) below the Median (4.5) and Mode (5).
