TASK 2: Instagram Influencer Follower Counts Skewness Analysis
===============================================================

1. Given Dataset (Follower counts in thousands):
   [5, 7, 8, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 95]
   Number of influencers (n) = 9

---------------------------------------------------------------
2. Mean Calculation:
---------------------------------------------------------------
   Formula: Mean = sum(x_i) / n
   Sum = 5 + 7 + 8 + 8 + 9 + 10 + 12 + 15 + 95 = 169
   Mean = 169 / 9
        = 18.78 (in thousands, i.e., 18,778 followers)

---------------------------------------------------------------
3. Median Calculation:
---------------------------------------------------------------
   The dataset is already arranged in ascending order:
   [5, 7, 8, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 95]
   Since n = 9 (odd), the median is the middle observation:
   Position = (9 + 1) / 2 = 5th observation
   Median = 9 (in thousands, i.e., 9,000 followers)

---------------------------------------------------------------
4. Comparison (Which is Higher?):
---------------------------------------------------------------
   - Mean   = 18.78k
   - Median = 9.00k
   
   Result: The **MEAN is significantly HIGHER than the MEDIAN** (18.78 > 9.00).

---------------------------------------------------------------
5. What This Tells You About the Skewness:
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Direction of Skew: The data is **RIGHT-SKEWED (Positively Skewed)**.
- Explanation: 
  The single celebrity influencer with 95k followers acts as a huge positive outlier. This outlier pulls the arithmetic mean up to 18.78k, even though 8 out of the 9 influencers (nearly 90% of the sample) have 15k followers or fewer. 
  Because the mean is strongly pulled in the positive direction away from the median (Mean > Median), the distribution stretches to the right.
